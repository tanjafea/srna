Foster story

A quiet dog who just wants a routine.

Luna spent her first year on a chain outside a village house before she was surrendered to a local shelter. She's cautious with new people at first, but she warms up fast once she trusts you — and once she does, she doesn't leave your side.

In foster care she's learned what a sofa is, what a leash walk feels like, and that the doorbell isn't a threat. She's food-motivated, easy to train, and thrives on a predictable daily rhythm.

She isn't asking for much. Just a door that stays open.
